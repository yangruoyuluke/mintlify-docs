### 10\+ Sets of Real Conversations

<Frame>
  Please provide typical conversation records from your actual business scenarios. These should be representative dialogues your salespeople have had in these scenarios. They must be complete and unedited, with at least 10 sets (the more, the better). The outcome can be a successful deal or not—the key is that these examples help your AI learn typical customer questions and corresponding responses.
</Frame>

### Company Documentation/Website

<Frame>
  This can be any company knowledge document that the AI needs to reference when responding. Supported formats include PDF, Excel, Word, and TXT files. Documents can be uploaded and managed in the backend.
</Frame>

### Dedicated AI Trainer/Contact Person

<Frame>
  Please designate a dedicated contact person who will oversee the onboarding process and AI training. This person will act as the AI’s “mentor” and have trainer permissions. They will provide ongoing guidance, answering questions or handling issues the AI encounters. Daily and weekly AI reports will also be sent to this contact by default. You may change the mentor at any time to ensure optimal AI training results.
</Frame>

### Sales Workflow Diagram

<Frame>
  Provide a workflow diagram illustrating your current sales process. This will help the AI understand the step-by-step operational flow, including key milestones and responsible departments or team members. For example, after Department A handles a process, the workflow moves to Department B at a certain point. Please supplement according to your actual situation. A swimlane diagram is recommended.
</Frame>

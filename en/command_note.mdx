# #Login Authentication

If you are a special collaborator for an AI role—such as a colleague, mentor, or supervisor—please enter `#auth` directly to your AI role for login authentication. This allows for further configuration, direct collaboration, and command issuance with the AI. Please keep your account credentials secure. After authentication, you can manage AI tasks directly via conversation, without needing to open your computer.

<Info>
  Admin authentication only needs to be completed once; no repeated authentication is required. In rare cases, such as backend upgrades, you may be prompted to re-authenticate.
</Info>

---

<Note>
  Note: The following assumes you have already completed #Login Authentication. If not, please do that step first.
</Note>

# #Training Mode

1. Enter `#Training Mode` in the AI chat to quickly add a single QA knowledge unit and supplement the AI’s knowledge base. Please follow the prompts step by step.
2. After entering `#Training Mode`, you’ll first be prompted to enter a question—please input a typical customer question at this stage.

<Tip>
  If possible, refine and improve your Q&A pairs. High-quality input will further boost the AI’s abilities.
</Tip>

3. Once you enter the question, you’ll be prompted to provide the answer. After completing both, you’ll receive a notification that the new QA has been recorded. The AI will then automatically exit training mode.

# #Mentor Authentication

Each AI role can be assigned one mentor for collaborative learning and escalation. When the AI encounters pending or complex issues, it will report them to the mentor for analysis. If you are the mentor, enter `#Mentor Authentication` to confirm your role. After entering the command, you’ll see a “Mentor authentication successful” notification.

<Tip>
  Each AI role can only have one mentor at a time. If another admin applies for this role, the mentor position will be reassigned.
</Tip>

# #Pause and #Resume Hosting

In special situations, such as holidays or other needs, you can quickly switch the AI’s active status by entering `#Pause Hosting` or `#Resume Hosting`.

# #Colleague Authentication

If you are a colleague of the AI role, use colleague authentication to confirm your identity. This helps the AI distinguish user roles. After login authentication, simply enter `#Colleague Authentication` in the chat.

<Info>
  Colleague authentication is crucial for smart group chats. If you wish to use intelligent group chat, please encourage as many colleagues as possible to complete colleague authentication.
</Info>

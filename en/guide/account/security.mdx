Login security is where you change the password you use to sign in to the MarsMind admin console. Enter your current password, set a new one, and confirm it; once the change goes through, your current session continues uninterrupted and the new password applies the next time you sign in. This page edits the account you sign in with; it does not change when you switch the "Selected window" on the left.

## When you'll need this

- You need to change your admin login password.
- You are worried someone else may know your password (for example, you signed in on a shared device) and want a new one as soon as possible.
- Your team's security policy requires you to update your password on a regular schedule.

## How to get there

Account settings in the lower-left → Login security. (You can also open [Profile](/en/guide/account/profile) or [AI identity access](/en/guide/account/ai-authorization) first, then click the tab at the top.)

When the page opens, the top shows three tabs — Profile / Login security / AI identity access — and it stays on Login security. Below is the "Update password" card with three fields — "Old Password", "New Password", and "Confirm Password" (each has an eye icon on its right) — and the "Update password" button at the bottom right.

## Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your current password">
    In the "Old Password" field of the "Update password" card (placeholder "Please enter old password"), enter the password you currently use to sign in.

    How to tell it worked: what you type shows as dots; click the eye icon on the right of the field to toggle between hidden and visible text and check what you entered.

    Note: whether the old password is correct cannot be checked before you submit. If the update fails, first check that you typed the old password correctly.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Set a new password">
    Enter the new password in the "New Password" field (placeholder "Please enter new password"). The rule shown under the field reads: "Use at least 6 characters and a different password from your current one."

    How to tell it worked: once the new password is at least 6 characters and different from the old one, this field no longer blocks submission; until then, the "Update password" button stays disabled.

    Note: the new-password rule only shows in the button state — if the password does not meet it, the button cannot be clicked and no error appears under the field.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Enter the new password again">
    In the "Confirm Password" field (placeholder "Please enter new password again"), type the new password once more, exactly as entered.

    How to tell it worked: when both entries match, no message appears below; as soon as Confirm Password is non-empty and does not match New Password, red text appears under the field right away — "The new passwords do not match." — and disappears once they match. When the two entries match and the other conditions are met, the "Update password" button becomes clickable.

    Note: letter case and spaces count as differences. If the mismatch message appears, use the eye icon to reveal both fields and compare them character by character.
  </Step>

  <Step title='Click "Update password" to submit'>
    Once all three fields are correct, click "Update password" at the bottom right of the card.

    How to tell it worked: while submitting, the button reads "Submitting..." and the three fields cannot be edited; on success, a "Password updated successfully!" message appears and the three fields are cleared (back to their placeholders). The password is now changed, and you do not need to sign in again.

    Note: there is no second confirmation — clicking the button starts the change immediately. If a "Password update failed, please try again" message appears, the change did not go through; check the old password and try again. If you switch menus or tabs or click the browser back button while editing, a "Discard unsaved changes?" confirmation appears first (description: "This page has unsaved changes. Leaving will discard them."): choose "Keep editing" to stay on this page and keep your input, or "Discard changes" to drop what you entered. You cannot leave while the update is in progress — the page shows "Wait for the current save or upload to finish before leaving."; refreshing or closing the page brings up the browser's own leave confirmation (without the "Keep editing" and "Discard changes" buttons), and leaving that way also discards what you entered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Checkpoints

- The "Password updated successfully!" message appeared and the three fields have been cleared.
- Your current session was not interrupted: the page does not ask you to sign in again, and you can keep using the admin console.
- The next time you sign in to the admin console, use the new password you just set.

## Important notes

<Warning>
Clicking "Update password" submits the change immediately — there is no second confirmation. A successful change does not interrupt your current session — do not assume it failed just because you were not signed out; go by whether the "Password updated successfully!" message appears.
</Warning>

<Info>
The password rule is the hint under the "New Password" field: "Use at least 6 characters and a different password from your current one." If the password is shorter than 6 characters or the same as the current one, the "Update password" button stays disabled and nothing is submitted.
</Info>

<Info>

</Tip>

## Frequently asked questions

### Do I need to sign in again after changing my password?

No. After a successful change your current session is not interrupted and the page does not ask you to sign in again; the three fields are cleared and "Password updated successfully!" appears. Use the new password the next time you sign in.

### Why is the "Update password" button disabled?

The button becomes clickable only when all three conditions are met: (1) "Old Password" is filled in; (2) "New Password" is at least 6 characters and different from the old password; (3) "Confirm Password" matches the new password. If you just clicked submit, the button shows "Submitting..." and the fields cannot be edited — wait for it to finish.

### What are the requirements for the new password?

The rule is the hint under the "New Password" field; until it is met, the "Update password" button stays disabled.

### I forgot my old password — can I change it here?

No. Changing the password on this page requires the old password, and the login page does not provide a self-service way to recover it. If you have forgotten your old password, contact an administrator for help.

### What happens if I switch to another page mid-edit?

As long as any field has content, a confirmation appears before you leave the page; you cannot leave while the update is in progress, and after a successful change the fields are cleared so the confirmation no longer appears.

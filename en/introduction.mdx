# **Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)**

**1. Who is MarsMind suitable for?**

MarsMind is designed for those seeking to improve customer service efficiency and quality while achieving real business results. It is mainly used by enterprise clients, helping reduce costs and boost efficiency, providing real-time native customer data for business optimization and monitoring. We’ve also recently opened access to individual users, empowering professionals to efficiently manage everything from lead identification to service completion. Our solutions cover internal training, customer service, sales, and more—dramatically increasing ROI and continually supporting your business growth.

**2. How do I integrate with MarsMind agents?**

MarsMind supports multi-channel integration, including web widgets, enterprise apps, WeChat ecosystem, WhatsApp, Line, and other mainstream platforms. Users can flexibly choose based on their needs for seamless integration. Both individuals and enterprises can register at [www.MarsMind.com](http://www.MarsMind.com), or contact a business manager for more information.

**3. What languages are supported?**

MarsMind supports automatic recognition and switching for up to 100 languages, meeting enterprise multilingual service needs and supporting global expansion.

**4. How is data security ensured?**

We strictly follow industry data security standards and use end-to-end encryption to keep customer data secure during transmission and storage. MarsMind also complies with all relevant laws and regulations, with signed compliance agreements to fully protect client privacy and compliance.

**5. How is the knowledge base updated and maintained?**

Clients can continually update and optimize the knowledge base through manual training, auto-learning, or targeted training by the MarsMind professional team—ensuring AI responses are accurate and business-aligned. We provide an admin portal for quick uploads and edits. You can let the agent naturally learn conversational style, perform one-by-one training, or use templates in the backend for bulk download, upload, and management of the knowledge base.

**6. Is manual intervention supported?**

Yes. MarsMind allows controllable agent management. Companies can start or pause agents as needed, with humans taking over at critical points to ensure service quality and satisfaction. There’s a built-in adaptive handover module: when a human responds, the AI automatically pauses and resumes after the human finishes, for a seamless experience.

**7. How long does it take to go live?**

Once basic configuration is complete after login, you can go live immediately. Core skills such as data analytics can be enabled the next day. With high-quality training, AI adaptation and optimization typically take effect within three weeks, achieving stable results depending on collaboration intensity. Basic features and standard operations can be mastered in about a week, and skill points take effect gradually. The AI will keep improving through self-learning even without human intervention. Custom development timelines depend on project scale and require individual assessment.

**8. Is technical support and training provided?**

MarsMind provides comprehensive technical support and training to help clients quickly master product use and ensure high agent efficiency and value. Refer to this document for operation guidance. For enterprise clients, we provide dedicated onboarding training and ongoing AI support.

**9. What is the pricing model? Is there a free trial?**

Our model includes a base subscription fee plus pay-as-you-go usage. Clients can flexibly choose modules and usage tiers as needed, and some features offer free trials.


> In most cases, the included quotas are sufficient. If you need more, additional value conversation packs are available.

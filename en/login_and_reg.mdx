# Account Login

1. Go to the official system address: [https://admin.marsmind.co/](https://admin.marsmind.co/#/login?redirect=%2Findex)
2. Enter your <Tooltip tip="If you’re unsure of your account name or password, please contact your account manager for assistance.">account name and password</Tooltip> to log in.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 23.31.17.png](/images/Screenshot2025-07-18at23.31.17.png)

<Note>
  If you need to use another language, click the language button in the top right corner. We currently support English, Chinese, and Japanese versions.
</Note>

# Account Registration

> If this is your first time using our system or you need to create a new account, click the center panel to switch to the registration page.

![Screenshot 2025-07-18 at 17.12.18.png](/images/Screenshot2025-07-18at17.12.18.png)

1. Enter your desired username, password, confirm your password, and your invitation code in sequence.
2. If you agree to the terms and conditions, check the box to confirm.
3. Click Register.

<Note>
  Currently, our system only supports registration via invitation code or through backend activation for enterprise accounts. Open registration is not supported at this time. If you’d like to learn more about our product, please email us at [sales@marsmind.co](mailto:sales@marsmind.co).
</Note>

# Product Setup

<Steps>
  <Step title="Understand the Product">
    Visit our [official website](marsmind.co), ask friends for a demo, or speak directly with MarsMind sales to learn more about product capabilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay Deposit">
    Enterprise: After confirming your role selection and configuration plan, pay the deposit to initiate role creation.

    Individual: You can start after payment. Currently, access is by invitation only. Please contact [sales@marsmind.co](mailto:sales@marsmind.co) to join the waiting list and request an invitation code.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Role Creation (Enterprise Only)">
    MarsMind will assist you with the entire role creation process. You will need to provide the following four elements:

    - 10+ sets of real conversation records
    - Company documentation and website information
    - A designated dedicated contact person
    - AI role business process flowchart
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go Live">
    Start experiencing the efficiency and empowerment of MarsMind’s AI Agent collaboration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# Activating from the Admin Portal

<Steps>

  <Step title="Go Live and Activate AI">
    Refer to [this document](/cn/run_avatar) to start your AI agent.
  </Step>
</Steps>

# **Core skills**

---

**1. Exclusive Intelligent Q&A**

Supports various forms of customer inquiries, such as text, voice, and images, and AI can understand context and provide coherent and accurate responses.

Skill effect:

Achieves up to 50 rounds of conversation context understanding (one question and one answer counts as one round); MultiModal Machine Learning interaction, where both images and voices are processed through AI recognition for thinking and response, adapting to diverse scenarios; Simulates human tone, attitude, and emotion to provide a natural and smooth customer communication experience; Natural tone enhances customer trust; Improves communication response speed and standardization of replies, strengthens customer stickiness, and reduces the pressure on human customer service.

How to use:

Users input questions through text, voice, or images, and the system automatically recognizes and generates answers without human intervention.

For example:

Customers upload product images on e-commerce platforms and ask "Are there any other colors for this clothing?" AI automatically recognizes the images and provides answers based on the product information.

---

**2. Controllable Hosting (Self-Adaptation Human-Machine Collaboration)**

Supports controlling the activation or suspension of AI at any time through commands, enabling flexible switching between human and AI. AI can automatically pause based on the status of human responses, supports customizing AI on-duty time, and enables human-machine collaboration in different time periods.

Skill effect:

Avoid AI misjudgment, ensure service quality; achieve human-machine collaboration, enhance customer experience; strengthen the enterprise's control over communication processes.

How to use:

The administrator enables/pauses AI through the background or instructions, or configures automatic switching rules and schedules, and the system automatically executes according to the configuration.

For example:

In legal consulting scenarios, when AI identifies a question related to legal provisions, it automatically transfers to human intervention, while other basic questions are managed by AI around the clock.

---

**3. Fast Automatic Learning**

AI continuously updates its Knowledge Base through manual training and automatic learning to enhance its answering capabilities.

Skill effect:

Knowledge Base is continuously updated; answers are more accurate, adapting to business changes; professional team targeted training is supported.

How to use:

Administrators can upload knowledge documents and annotate content through the backend, and the system automatically collects and learns from historical conversation content to continuously iterate the Knowledge Base.

For example:

Whenever a company launches a new product, the administrator uploads the product manual and supplements the Q & A template. AI can automatically learn new knowledge and use it for subsequent customer answers.

---

**4. Automatic Language Switching**

Automatically switches based on the customer's language, supports up to 100 languages, and ensures smooth communication.

Skill effect:

Seamless multi-language switching; natural and fluent language expression; support for enterprise internationalization services.

How to use:

Customers can input any supported language, and the system will automatically recognize and reply with the corresponding language, without the need to manually switch settings.

For example:

German users ask questions in German, and the AI automatically recognizes and responds in German, allowing customers to switch seamlessly without awareness.

---

**5. Periodic Automatic Statistical Report**

Regularly generate customer communication data reports, synchronize daily and weekly work performance Statistical Data.

Skill effect:

Automatically statistics communication data; assist business decision-making and service optimization; save manual statistics time.

How to use:

The system automatically generates data reports on a regular basis, and administrators can view them in the background or receive them via email.

For example:

Every Monday morning, the system automatically sends statistical reports such as last week's customer service conversation volume, Customer Satisfaction Score, and hot issue rankings to the operations team's email.

---

**6. Ask if you don't understand**

When AI encounters a question that cannot be answered, it automatically initiates a consultation with the designated supervisor to ensure accurate response. The teacher can choose to reply to AI and let AI reply to the customer, or manually reply directly.

Skill effect:

Improve answer accuracy; respond quickly to complex questions; ensure professionalism and customer satisfaction.

How to use:

Once enabled, when AI is unable to answer, it will automatically initiate a tutor consultation without manual intervention. Tutors can directly handle it in the backend.

For example:

When AI encounters difficult medical consultations, it automatically pushes them to the doctor experts in the background. After the doctor handles it, AI then feeds back the results to the customer.

---

**7. Smart Query**

Automatically summarize customer communication content, classify and organize historical issues to facilitate analysis and improvement.

Skill effect:

Quickly categorize issues; support multi-level classification management; improve service efficiency and quality.

How to use:

Administrators can search historical communication data and typical issues by keyword, tag, or category in the backend.

For example:

The Product Manager can search for all relevant customer historical issues with a single click using the keyword "payment failure", assisting in business decision-making and product optimization.

**8. Knowledge Adjustment (Coming Soon)**

Supports content updates and reshaping of the Knowledge Base to maintain information accuracy and timeliness.

Skill effect:

Continuously optimize the Knowledge Base; adapt to business changes; ensure the accuracy of responses.

How to use:

For example:

When enterprise policies change, administrators can adjust Knowledge Base entries at any time, and AI immediately synchronizes new content to respond to users.

**9. Automatically approve friend requests**

Automatically receive potential customers through friend requests to avoid missing business opportunities.

Skill effect:

Capture more business opportunities; save manual operation time; maintain the integrity of customer resources.

How to use:

After the function is turned on, the system automatically accepts new friend requests without manual operation.

For example:

When a new user adds the enterprise customer service WeChat, the system automatically approves the friend request, ensuring that the enterprise does not miss any potential customer resources.

# **Advanced skills**

---

**10. Group chat support (replies available without @)**

Proactively monitor issues within the customer group, respond promptly, and enhance group interaction.

Skill effect:

Avoid omitting customer issues; improve customer satisfaction; enhance the active level of group chats.

How to use:

The system automatically takes effect after enabling group chat monitoring, without human intervention.

For example:

In the enterprise VIP customer WeChat group, when a member raises a product question, AI automatically listens and quickly responds professionally, enhancing the group's active level and corporate image.

---

**11. Take the initiative to follow up**

When a customer does not reply for a long time, AI will automatically generate a personalized narrative based on the last communication topic for proactive follow-up, confirming user intentions and service needs, and continuously promoting communication.

Skill effect:

Actively maintain customer relationships; enhance communication consistency and service warmth; reduce the pressure of manual follow-up; improve customer conversion rate and satisfaction.

How to use:

Administrators can set the time and frequency of follow-up, and the system automatically detects customers' non-response and generates customized follow-up content without manual intervention.

For example:

E-commerce customers inquire about products but have not placed an order yet. The AI automatically sends a follow-up message the next day: "Hello, the dress you inquired about yesterday is still in stock. Do you need to keep it for you?" and intelligently judges the user's intention.

---

**12. Report on Specified Situations (Escalate to Manual)**

When a critical situation occurs, AI notifies the person in charge immediately to ensure timely handling.

Skill effect:

Provide real-time early warnings for important events; respond quickly to customers' special needs; and improve customer satisfaction.

How to use:

After the system automatically detects anomalies or critical events, it will push them to the person in charge through messages or emails.

For example:

When AI detects that a customer complaint has escalated or that the same issue has been repeatedly consulted multiple times, it automatically pushes an early warning to the operations supervisor's WeChat or corporate email to ensure timely manual handling.

---

**13. Smart Tags (Limited to Pro Users)**

Automatically extract tags from customer conversations to help companies accurately identify customer characteristics.

Skill effect:

Build accurate user portraits; support personalized services and marketing; improve customer conversion rates.

How to use:

Automatically takes effect after activation, and the background can view and filter conversation tags and user portraits.

For example:

AI automatically tags frequent users with "high potential customers" to help marketing departments with precise marketing.

---

**14. Enterprise Data Access (Customized on Demand)**

Deeply integrate enterprise systems to enable rapid query of information such as orders, user status, and logistics.

Skill effect:

Real-time access to system data; improve customer response speed; meet complex business needs.

How to use:

Customized integration is required. After connecting with the internal system of the enterprise, AI can automatically query and reply to customer-related data.

For example:

Customers inquire about the logistics progress of their orders on WeChat, and AI automatically invokes Enterprise Resource Planning to reply in real-time with the latest logistics status.

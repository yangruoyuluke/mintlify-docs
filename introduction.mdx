# About MarsMind

MarsMind is a technology company specializing in serious commercial artificial intelligence, mainly providing standardized agents tailored to client scenarios, focusing on enhancing the interactive experience in client service scenarios, and improving client service and sales efficiency. Currently, the main products offered include:

1. Internal Training Assistant - An intelligent assistant specifically designed for internal employee training within enterprises, which combines the enterprise's unique Knowledge Base and business processes to provide a personalized and interactive training experience. This intelligent assistant simulates Q&A and operational guidance in real work scenarios, helping employees quickly master job skills and corporate culture, thereby improving training efficiency and learning outcomes.
2. Customer Service Chatbot - Completes the enterprise's exclusive knowledge Q&A, purely generated by artificial intelligence, suitable for customers with ultra-cost-effective requirements for customer service scenarios.
3. Sales Agent - Completes the specified sales tasks from start to finish, suitable for clients with specific requirements for the complete execution of sales scenarios.

# MarsMind Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. Which users are MarsMind's products suitable for?

MarsMind is mainly suitable for users who hope to improve the efficiency and quality of client service and achieve certain results. One type of user is enterprise users, for whom it can reduce costs and increase efficiency in client service, and obtain real-time client native data statistics, facilitating the optimization and monitoring of existing business. Another type is that we are also recently opening services for individual users, providing in-depth professional users who hope to handle client service more efficiently with a complete closed-loop client service from sales lead quality identification to completion. Our solutions are applicable to various business scenarios such as internal training, customer service, and sales, which can greatly enhance your ROI and continuously accompany your business to grow together.

### 2. How to connect to MarsMind's agent?

MarsMind supports multi-channel access, including mainstream communication platforms such as the official website dialog box, enterprise APP, WeChat ecosystem, WhatsApp, Line, etc. Customers can choose the appropriate access method according to their own needs to achieve seamless connection. ### 3. Which languages does the AI agent support?

MarsMind supports automatic recognition and switching of up to 100 languages, meeting the multilingual service needs of enterprises and helping them expand into international markets.

### 4. How does MarsMind ensure data security?

We strictly adhere to industry data security standards and adopt end-to-end encryption technology to ensure the security of customer data during transmission and storage. Meanwhile, MarsMind complies with relevant laws and regulations, fully signs agreements with you, and safeguards customer privacy and compliance.

### 5. How is the Knowledge Base updated and maintained?

Customers can continuously update and optimize the Knowledge Base through various methods, including manual training, automatic learning, and targeted training from the RuFit professional team, ensuring that the answers of AI agents are accurate and meet business requirements. Meanwhile, we provide a management backend for you to quickly upload and revise your Knowledge Base. You can choose to let your agent gradually learn your daily communication style naturally, or conduct single-round training on it, or log in to our product backend, where we will provide templates for you to perform complete downloading, uploading, and more comprehensive Knowledge Base management operations.

### 6. Does it support manual intervention?

Yes, MarsMind provides controllable hosting capabilities. Enterprises can turn on or pause AI agents at any time as needed, with human intervention taking over to ensure service quality and customer satisfaction at critical moments. Meanwhile, we have also built-in a Self-Adaptation intervention module. When you reply, the AI will automatically pause, and after the reply conversation ends, it will automatically resume hosting.
